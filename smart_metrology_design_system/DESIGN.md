## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of modern Indian civic governance and precision enterprise technology. Built specifically for AI-powered legal verification, automated computer vision inspections, and evidentiary reporting under regulatory statutes, the aesthetic pairs rigorous administrative authority with crisp, high-efficiency SaaS ergonomics.

The visual direction balances:
- **Administrative Trust & Sovereign Utility:** Clean, disciplined, high-clarity structural canvases reminiscent of national digital public infrastructure (India Stack, Digital India) layered with contemporary institutional authority.
- **Precision Computer Vision:** Sharp, confident UI primitives engineered for telemetry, confidence scorings, bounding-box diagnostics, and real-time inference displays.
- **Modern Civic Ergonomics:** Frictionless white and slate backdrops, disciplined hairline structural borders, high-contrast typography, and purposeful national accents (subtle saffron and tricolor-inspired status signifiers).

## Colors

The system operates primarily in a crisp, high-visibility light theme optimized for desk-bound enforcement officers, lab verification specialists, and mobile field inspectors. Deep navy tones convey governmental authority and frame top-level system navigation, while electric blue drives tactical actions. Semantic verification tokens provide instant, uncompromised clarity on regulatory compliance.

### Palette Roles & Specific Usage:
- **Command & Structure (Deep Navy):** `#0F172A` and `#0B192C` provide anchors for the primary application header, formal report titles, and structural sidebars.
- **System Action (Electric / Royal Blue):** `#2563EB` (hover: `#1D4ED8`) commands interaction focus, inspection triggers, and active data filters.
- **Compliance Validation (Indian Green):** `#16A34A` (dark: `#15803D`, background tint: `#F0FDF4`) denotes confirmed adherence to packaged commodity legal criteria and verified declarations.
- **Violation Severity (Alert Crimson):** `#DC2626` (dark: `#B91C1C`, tint: `#FEF2F2`) signals mandatory missing declarations, deceptive packaging alerts, and evidentiary flags.
- **Diagnostic Warning (Amber Gold):** `#F59E0B` (tint: `#FFFBEB`) flags OCR readability hazards, blur warnings, and pending officer confirmations.
- **Civic Accent (Saffron Orange):** `#EA580C` is applied judiciously as a sovereign accent on hero focal points, badge ribbons, and initiative markers without overpowering functional status colors.
- **Surfaces & Neutral Hierarchy:** Primary background uses `#F8FAFC`, card surfaces sit on `#FFFFFF`, input/table wells leverage `#F1F5F9`, and borders maintain a disciplined `#E2E8F0`. Text tiers descend from `#0F172A` (primary text) through `#475569` (secondary text) down to `#94A3B8` (subdued metadata).

## Typography

The typographic system combines the geometric authority and open apertures of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and high-level analytical markers with the utilitarian legibility of **Inter** for dense evidence displays, legal citations, and metric grids.

- **Headlines & Section Headers:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural firmness, high legibility at glance speed, and executive authority. Tightened letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) prevents loose text blocks in data-dense headers.
- **Legal Declarations & Tabular Body:** Inter ensures uncompromised optical clarity when reading complex manufacturer details, multi-decimal weights (`Net Qty: 1 kg`), statutory dates, and MRP currency indicators (`₹45.00`).
- **Data Callouts & Bounding Box Labels:** Set strictly in bold uppercase or high-contrast semibold (`label-sm`), ensuring field inspectors can instantly verify confidence outputs against commodity packaging imagery.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around an 8px spatial grid with a responsive 12-column layout for administrative desktops and a 4-column layout for handheld field tablets and mobile enforcement scanners.

### Layout Philosophy:
- **Maximum Width:** High-density dashboards and verification interfaces conform to a maximum bounding container of `1280px` (`80rem`) centered within wide screens to guarantee ocular comfort during continuous record audits.
- **Split Scanning Workspace:** In active audit screens, use a fixed 50/50 desktop split: the left panel hosts the raw visual asset with bounding box overlays and target crosshairs; the right panel presents parsed OCR fields, legal validation rules, and compliance checklists.
- **Responsive Stacking:** At viewports under `1024px`, the layout shifts to a vertical step-through flow where camera/image ingestion takes precedence, followed by interactive verification sheets and action buttons fixed to the viewport base.
- **Rhythm & Information Density:** Analytical widgets maintain a strict internal padding of `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (`spacing-lg`), balancing administrative density with crisp scannability.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using **tonal elevation combined with precision hairline borders**. This produces an authoritative, clinical software atmosphere where data points take absolute visual precedence.

### Elevation Levels:
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background foundation (`#F8FAFC`), entirely flat without shadows.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Data Panels):** `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by a crisp `1px` solid outline in `#E2E8F0`, accompanied by an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover Targets & Interactive Panels):** Used on interactive audit cards, repository rows on hover, and active modal frames. Elevation is expressed via: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
4. **Level 3 (Inspection Overlays, Tooltips, & Prompts):** `#FFFFFF` surface with an elevated shadow of `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
5. **Computer Vision Overlays:** Bounding-box tags on packaging images use translucent backdrops (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) bordered by the respective validation color (`#2563EB` for neutral detection, `#16A34A` for valid declarations, `#DC2626` for flagged defects).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry balance. Rounded corners soften analytical software interfaces while maintaining the structural rigidity expected of legal, state-level instruments.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Standardized corner radius of `0.75rem` (`12px`) up to `1rem` (`16px`), providing balanced containment for legal metric summaries and scan previews.
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, & Select Wells:** Configured with `0.5rem` (`8px`) radius to maintain clean grid alignment across dense input groups.
- **Status Pills, Tags, & Vision Badges:** Configured with full pill rounding (`9999px`) or `0.375rem` (`6px`) micro-rounding for bounding box indicators, allowing immediate optical distinction from structural rectangular containers.
- **Inspection Camera Frames:** Center mobile scanner frames use a polished `2rem` (`32px`) handset bezel frame to convey modern native hardware scanning.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign CTA:** Deep Navy background (`#0F172A`) or Royal Blue (`#2563EB`) with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography, semibold `14px`, `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`. On hover, transitions to a rich hover tint (`#1D4ED8` or `#1E293B`) with a subtle `Level 1` lift.
- **Secondary / Evidence CTA:** Transparent or `#FFFFFF` background with a `1px` border in `#E2E8F0` (`hover:border-slate-400`), text `#0F172A`, subtle slate hover fill (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Destructive / Non-Compliance CTA:** Solid `#DC2626` text or fill, designed for explicit officer rejection or violation logging.

### 2. Status Badges & Compliance Pills
- **Valid / Compliant (Pass):** Background `#F0FDF4`, text `#15803D`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`. Features a checkmark icon indicator.
- **Non-Compliant (Fail / Violation):** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C`, border `1px solid #FECACA`. Accompanied by an alert triangle icon.
- **Verification Required / Review:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`. Accompanied by an eye/clock icon.

### 3. Inspection Cards & Evidence Modules
- **Overview Stat Cards:** Minimal `#FFFFFF` tile, `1px` border (`#E2E8F0`), housing an upper stat label (`body-sm`), a bold value counter (`headline-lg`), an accompanying directional trend pill, and an optional micro-sparkline.
- **Violation Analysis Module:** Two-column evidence view pairing the cropped source camera slice on the left alongside the rule reference (e.g., *Rule 6(1)(a) Legal Metrology Rules, 2011*), extracted value, confidence percentage score, and officer action toggle switches.

### 4. Bounding Box Vision Overlays
- Rendered directly over captured commodity packaging graphics.
- Outlined with a high-visibility `2px` stroke (Royal Blue `#2563EB`, Compliant Green `#16A34A`, or Violation Red `#DC2626`).
- Each box is paired with an anchor pointer badge: white pill with `11px` bold text, high contrast shadow, and an icon confirming the detected attribute (`Net Quantity`, `MRP`, `Consumer Care`, `Country of Origin`).

### 5. Form Controls & Search Filtering
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** `#FFFFFF` background with a `1px` solid border (`#CBD5E1`), text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focused state: border `#2563EB`, with a soft ring glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Selection Tables (Product Repository):** High-density tabular layout. Header row set in `#F8FAFC` with uppercase `11px` semibold labels. Alternating light row borders (`#F1F5F9`), highlighting cleanly on hover (`#F8FAFC`).

### 6. Workflow Step Indicators
- Connected horizontal progression circles (`01 Scan` → `02 OCR` → `03 Extract` → `04 Validate` → `05 Report`).
- Active and completed states use royal blue with icon indicators; unvisited states use muted slate nodes connected by a `2px` dashed track (`#E2E8F0`).
